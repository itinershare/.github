# ItinerShare

Travel planning should be collaborative, simple, and actually enjoyable.

We’re building tools that make sharing travel plans as easy as sending a message.

ItinerShare focuses on lightweight, mobile-first software that helps people organise trips together without spreadsheets, screenshots, or long message threads.

---

## Our Product

We’re building a Flutter-based itinerary sharing app that lets users:

- Create structured itineraries
- Add attractions, restaurants, and travel stages
- Schedule activities
- Keep trip notes in one place
- Share plans with others

Our goal is to remove friction from group travel planning.

---

## Our Approach

We believe good software should be:

- Simple to understand
- Fast to use
- Reliable offline
- Easy to maintain
- Built iteratively

We prefer small, well-designed features over large, complicated systems.
